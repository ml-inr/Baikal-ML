# Prefilter: DA-repair retrain vs no-DANN control

The new prefilter pair, both trained 2026-11-08/09 on hard labels with the
DA-repair recipe. The two runs share the `hard_nu_labels` source dataset, a
muatm-only adversarial source (`domain_source: muatm`), `k_steps=10`, a
discriminator refresh every 5 epochs, LayerNorm + instance noise in the
discriminator and the same invariance probe; seed 42, architecture and `exp.h5`
target are also the same. The only difference is the adversarial strength:

| model | run | `lambda_scheduler` |
|---|---|---|
| DA | `da_prefilter_numu_261108_1943_Qclip_hardlabels_FocalLoss_muOnlyDA_k10_refresh5_probe` | `delayed_constant`, 0.003 from epoch 2 |
| control | `da_prefilter_numu_261109_1845_Qclip_hardlabels_FocalLoss_lambda0_control_k10_refresh5_probe` | `constant`, 0.0 |

The control still trains the discriminator (`k_steps`, refresh) and still runs
the probe; the encoder receives no domain gradient. This follows the previous
pair in `report_260907_0820`, which compared `max_lambda` 0.05 against 0.0 with
the older soft-label model.

## Predictions

Made 2026-11-11 with the same commands recorded in `run_predictions.sh`:

| source | events per model | what |
|---|---:|---|
| `mc_merged` | 14,292,070 | held-out canonical parts only: 10M muatm, 3M nuatm, all 1.29M nue2 |
| `exp_full` | 133,294,745 | all 29 parts |

Training parts are removed at scoring time by
`inference/prefilter/predict_mc.py --exclude-trained-parts`, which reads the
checkpoint's `source_domain.npy_dir` and drops all 952 h5 parts the dataset was
built from (432 muatm + 187 nuatm + 333 nue2). This is the prefilter counterpart
of `reader.NOT_TRAINED_PARTS`: the prefilter NPY dataset records no
`h5_part_keys.npy`, so per-event exclusion is impossible and whole parts are the
finest handle. The held-out part lists are in `scored_parts/`; their intersection
with the 952 training parts is empty.

The MC sample is deliberately reduced (10M/3M/1.29M) to keep scoring cheap; the
exp_full prediction is complete. The figures are statistically limited by that
choice.

## First look (h8s3 on MC truth, suppression = 1e-3)

From the executed notebooks; each model is evaluated at **its own** threshold
that puts muatm at a pass fraction of 1e-3.

| model | threshold | nuatm | nue2 | weighted nuatm | weighted nue2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| DA | 0.8027 | 0.9869 | 0.9789 | 0.9748 | 0.9816 |
| control | 0.7920 | 0.9858 | 0.9763 | 0.9721 | 0.9788 |

The DA model is marginally better at matched muatm suppression (0.1–0.3
percentage points on the neutrino efficiencies); the score scales differ, so
comparing at a fixed score would have been misleading. On the reduced MC sample
the h8s3-truth counts are 2,590,771 per model (muatm 1,900,554, nuatm 113,041,
nue2 577,176); the sig-noise h8s3 counts are muatm 967,822 and exp_full
3,180,681 for both models (the cuts do not depend on the model).

Figure 4 is the exp_full/muatm shape ratio, **not a rate** (no live-time
normalisation). Its cumulative panel shows the control's ratio higher at
moderate thresholds (~1.58 against ~1.42) while the DA's ratio rises above it in
the last score bins (~2.5 against ~1.8); the per-bin tail is statistically thin,
so the cumulative shape is the stable part.

Figure 5 reparameterizes the same cumulative ratio by the **fraction of muatm
above the threshold**, matching the nu-classifier survival template
(`HIGH_SCORE_EXCESS/template_score_survival.ipynb`): Wilson 95% on x,
delta-method ±1σ on y, both axes logarithmic. At suppression $10^{-3}$ the
ratio is ~1.3 (DA) against ~1.5 (control); the control's curve is above from
~$10^{-4}$ to ~$10^{-1}$, while the DA rises above it only at the extreme
low-fraction end. Both are shape ratios, not rates.

## Caveats

* **Weights.** `EventWeight` is applied within a class only; whether it is
  comparable *between* classes is `assumed`, not checked. Weighted and
  unweighted panels are drawn side by side.
* **`nue2` is astrophysical nu_mu with an E^-2 spectrum**, not electron neutrinos.
* **No live-time normalisation** exists for this MC, so figure 4 is a shape
  ratio and its absolute level is not a rate.
* **The MC classes occupy disjoint zenith hemispheres**: muatm 94.2–179.9 deg,
  both neutrino classes 0.11–90.00 deg. The separation is easier than a real
  sample would offer; logged in `doc/claims_log.md`.
* **Two exp_full runs** are dropped by `reader.NOT_EXCLUDED` (four channels
  emitting up to 3169.8 p.e. against a median hit of 0.97; evidence in
  `inference/reader/spec.py`). `EXCLUDE_BAD_EXP_RUNS` in each notebook turns this
  off; the counts either way are printed.
* **MC statistics**: the prediction set is the reduced 10M/3M/1.29M sample, not
  the full 127M canonical one.
